3 repos that make content

No voice artist, no designer, no editor: GPT-SoVITS speaks in your voice, ComfyUI makes images and AI video on your own computer, and OpenCut edits it all for free.

Start setup ↓

What you need

  • A Windows or Mac computer. ComfyUI's video models want a good graphics card (NVIDIA, AMD, Intel or Apple Silicon).
  • 7-Zip to unpack the GPT-SoVITS Windows package.
  • A clean recording of your own voice, about 5 seconds, plus the exact words you said in it.
  • Set up one tool at a time. They don't depend on each other.

Setup, step by stepcopy each command with the button on the right

01 · Your voiceGPT-SoVITS

  1. Download the Windows package

    Get the integrated package (a big .7z file) and unpack it with 7-Zip. Tested on Windows 10 and newer.

  2. Start it

    Open the unpacked folder and double-click go-webui.bat. The GPT-SoVITS web app opens in your browser.

  3. Make it talk in your voice

    In the inference (TTS) part of the app, add your ~5-second clip and type exactly what you said in it. Then type the new text and generate. For better quality, the app can also fine-tune on about 1 minute of your audio.

  4. Mac or Linux instead

    Install Miniconda, download the repo, then in its folder:

    conda create -n GPTSoVits python=3.10
conda activate GPTSoVits
bash install.sh --device CPU --source HF

    On Linux with an NVIDIA card use --device CU128. On a Mac the project uses the CPU for training on purpose: Mac GPU training gives much worse quality. github.com/RVC-Boss/GPT-SoVITS

02 · Images + AI videoComfyUI

  1. Install the desktop app

    Download it from comfy.org/download (Windows and Mac) and open it. Linux users follow the manual install in the README.

  2. Start from a template

    Pick one of the built-in templates (start with an image one). It tells you which model file to download, then press Run.

  3. Keep it offline

    The core downloads nothing unless you ask. It also has optional paid partner nodes; leave them off and everything runs on your own computer for free. github.com/Comfy-Org/ComfyUI

03 · EditingOpenCut

  1. Open it in your browser

    Go to opencut.app and open the editor. Nothing to install, and your videos stay on your device.

  2. Edit

    Create a project, drop in your clips and voiceover, cut them on the timeline and export.

  3. Run it yourself (optional)

    It's open source under MIT, so developers can run their own copy from github.com/OpenCut-app/OpenCut.

Heads up: clone only your own voice, or someone who said yes, and never use a cloned voice to trick people. GPT-SoVITS speaks English, Japanese, Korean, Chinese and Cantonese; Hindi isn't supported yet. OpenCut is an early beta that's being rewritten from the ground up, so keep your original clips. ComfyUI video models are heavy; on a weak graphics card, start with images.

Stuck?

GPT-SoVITS won't open

Make sure the whole .7z unpacked (it's large), then run go-webui.bat from inside the unpacked folder. Keep the black window open while you use the app.

ComfyUI is very slow or runs out of memory

Video models need a lot of graphics memory. Try an image template first, or a smaller video model.

Is OpenCut a full CapCut replacement?

Not yet. It's good for basic editing today; more features are coming with the rewrite.
